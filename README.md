# Case study Sword health

Sword Health is an AI-powered healthcare provider that delivers clinical-grade phys-
ical therapy directly to patients’ homes. Its core solution uses wearable motion-tracking
sensors and an AI “digital therapist” (named Phoenix) to guide users through exercises
with real-time biofeedback. Licensed Doctors of Physical Therapy (DPTs) monitor pa-
tient progress through a remote dashboard, intervening when necessary. By replacing
inconvenient, high-cost clinic visits with a scalable digital platform, Sword has saved
its clients over $1 billion in healthcare costs. This analysis explores how the company
transitioned from a Porto-based research project to a $4 billion global leader, utilizing
an unconventional academic incubation strategy and an innovative “at-risk” business
model.
